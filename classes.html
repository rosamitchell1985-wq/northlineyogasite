Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any experience or equipment?
None at all. Beginners are our specialty. All you need is a sturdy chair without wheels, comfortable clothing, and a little space to move.
What if I have arthritis, a joint replacement, or balance issues?
Our instructors are trained in senior adaptations. Every pose has a seated or supported version, and we always encourage you to move within a pain-free range. Please check with your doctor first, as with any exercise program.
How do I join a live class?
After joining, you'll receive an email with a simple link. Click it at class time from any computer, tablet, or smartphone — or call the toll-free number we provide and follow along by audio.
Can I cancel my membership?
Yes. Cancel anytime from your account page or by calling 1-800-555-0142. Monthly memberships stay active until the end of the billing period. See our Cancellation & Refund Policy for details.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes. Payments are processed over an encrypted connection, and we never store your full card number on our servers. See our Privacy Policy.
