Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

30-Day Money-Back Promise

If you're not completely happy with Northline Yoga during your first 30 days of membership, just contact us and we will refund your initial payment in full — no questions asked.

How to Cancel

  • Call us free at 1-800-555-0142 (Mon–Fri, 8am–6pm CT), or
  • Email hello@northlineyoga.site from the email address on your account, or
  • Use the cancellation link in any of our emails.

What Happens When You Cancel

  • Monthly Membership: Your cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing month. You keep full access until then, and no further charges are made.
  • Annual Membership: Your cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing year. No further annual charges are made.

Refunds After the First 30 Days

Beyond the 30-day money-back promise, membership fees are non-refundable for partial billing periods. If you experience a billing error or unauthorized charge, contact us right away and we will make it right.

Billing Questions

Our team is happy to help with any billing or cancellation question: 1-800-555-0142 or hello@northlineyoga.site.
