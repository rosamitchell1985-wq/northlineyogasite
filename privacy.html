Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Northline Yoga ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website northlineyoga.site and provides online yoga classes and guided wellness content for seniors. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Contact information you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, and billing ZIP code when you join or contact us.
  • Payment information processed securely by our payment partners. We never store your full card number on our servers.
  • Usage information such as pages visited and class attendance, which helps us improve our services.
  • Device and log information such as browser type and general location (city/state level) used for security and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide your membership, class access, and customer support.
  • To send class reminders, schedule changes, and account notices by email, text, or phone.
  • To process payments and manage renewals and cancellations.
  • To improve our classes and website based on aggregate, non-identifying usage data.

We will never sell your personal information to third parties. We do not share your information except with trusted service providers (such as payment processors and email delivery services) who are required to protect it, or when required by law.

3. Text and Email Communications

By providing your phone number, you consent to receive service-related messages (such as class reminders). Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to end text messages at any time or contact us to update your preferences.

4. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences (such as text size) and to keep our site secure. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the site will continue to work.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard encryption (SSL/TLS) for all data in transit and limit access to personal information to staff who need it to serve you. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

6. Your Rights

You may request to review, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. Residents of certain states (including California under the CCPA/CPRA) may have additional rights regarding their personal data. To exercise any right, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the current version. Material changes will be announced by email before they take effect.
