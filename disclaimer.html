Health Disclaimer

Last updated: September 1, 2026

General Wellness Content Only

The classes, pose guides, videos, and articles provided by Northline Yoga are for general educational and wellness purposes only. They are not medical advice, and they are not a substitute for professional medical care, diagnosis, or treatment.

Consult Your Physician

Always consult your doctor or qualified healthcare provider before starting any new exercise program, including yoga — especially if you have or suspect you have any medical condition, such as heart disease, high blood pressure, glaucoma, osteoporosis, arthritis, a recent surgery, or joint replacement, or if you take medications that affect balance or heart rate.

Practice at Your Own Risk

Exercise involves inherent risks. You participate in Northline Yoga classes voluntarily and assume responsibility for your own safety. Never force or strain into any pose, stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath, or chest discomfort, and seek medical attention if symptoms persist.

No Guaranteed Results

Individual results vary. Nothing on this website guarantees improvements in balance, mobility, pain, or any health outcome.

Questions?

Talk with your healthcare provider about whether gentle yoga is right for you. If you have questions about our classes, we're glad to help: 1-800-555-0142 or hello@northlineyoga.site.
